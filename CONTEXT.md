# GymHop Waitlist

Pre-launch validation for a credit-based day-pass platform for neighborhood gyms in Vietnam. This context covers the landing page, waitlist capture, and market survey.

## Language

**Credit**:
A single unit of entry. One credit redeems one workout session.
_Avoid_: pass, day-pass, token, free workout pass

**Pass Bundle**:
A pack of credits bought together (1 single, 10, or 15).
_Avoid_: pass (alone), credit pack

**Check-in**:
Redemption of credits via QR scan at the gym desk. One tap can burn N credits for N people.
_Avoid_: scan, checkin, visit

**Partner gym**:
A neighborhood budget gym in the GymHop network.
_Avoid_: gym cỏ, local gym, budget gym (in code and UI copy outside of marketing)

**Waitlister**:
A person who completed the survey. Name required; contact (phone/Zalo/email) optional — contacts earn 2 bonus credits on launch.
_Avoid_: lead, user, early access user

**Qualified lead**:
A Waitlister who left contact info and can be reached on launch.
_Avoid_: surveyed user, converted lead

**Segment**:
New (< 3 months) vs experienced gym-goer, asked first to branch the survey.
_Avoid_: cohort, persona

**Queue position**:
Mock early-access rank shown at confirmation (base + local increment for v1).
_Avoid_: rank, placement

**Referral**:
A Waitlister sharing their unique link; both sides earn 1 bonus credit on launch.
_Avoid_: invite, share bonus
